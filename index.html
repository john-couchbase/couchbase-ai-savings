Methodology & assumptions

How savings are calculated

Caching reduces repeat model calls. Memory, retrieval, and tool search reduce input tokens. Reductions are combined rather than added.

What the estimate includes

Model costs only. Couchbase, implementation, and any existing GPU commitments must be considered separately.

Where the assumptions come from

Defaults draw on published research and product benchmarks. Replace them with measurements from your own workload when available.
